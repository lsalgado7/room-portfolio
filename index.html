~ My Work ~

Agentic Research Assistant

Tech Skills: Python, CrewAI, Google Gemini, Streamlit, Pydantic
Soft Skills: System Architecture, Prompt Engineering

I developed an autonomous multi-agent system that acts as a "Database Finder" for researchers. It orchestrates a crew of specialized AI agents to discover and validate open-source datasets. Built with Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite and Pydantic, the system ensures zero-hallucination in technical metadata. It generates two outputs: structured JSON for data ingestion and markdown guides for research strategy. I am currently adding a "Data Workbench" feature to automate cleaning and EDA directly through the Streamlit UI.

Sentinelle

Tech Skills: Python, SQLite & SQLCipher, Rust
Soft Skills: Prioritization, strategic execution, regular communications

My teammate and I are developing a privacy-centric self-care app that transforms a user's digital footprint into actionable behavioral insights. To ensure total data security, I architected a backend using Rust, SQLite, and SQLCipher, keeping all user data encrypted and strictly local.

I leverage Pandas, Scikit-learn, and Plotly for the analytical engine, providing users with sophisticated visualizations of their digital habits. The entire suite is built with Flet, allowing us to ship the project as a high-performance, cross-platform executable.

Project Larry

Tech Skills: Python, Machine Learning, Git/GitHub
Soft Skills: Organization during crunch time, adaptability

For QuackHacks 2024, my team developed an interactive game designed to educate users on the subtler side of data collection. We used machine learning for webcam-based motion tracking to create a "Just Dance" style experience.

Upon finishing, the game states it could have been logging the user's behavioral data, serving as a hands-on lesson in digital privacy. Our project placed 5th out of 25 teams in a field of over 100 participants.
